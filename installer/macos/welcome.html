MacDanial Hills Mastering Compressor

by Basemental

Two mastering compressors stacked in one plug-in:

  • Model A – dual-stage Optical + Discrete compressor with Nickel / Iron / Steel output transformers
  • Model B – Class-A edition with input transformer and Class-A discrete VCA

The installer adds the Audio Unit (for Logic Pro) and the VST3 to your system plug-in folders. After installing, restart your DAW. In Logic, the plug-in appears under Audio Units › Basemental.
