MacDanial Hills Mastering Compressor

by Basemental

Two mastering compressors stacked in one plug-in:

  • Model A – dual-stage Optical + Discrete compressor with Nickel / Iron / Steel output transformers
  • Model B – Class-A edition with input transformer and Class-A discrete VCA

The installer adds the Audio Unit (Logic Pro, GarageBand), the VST3 (Ableton Live, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper and others) and, when included, the AAX version for Pro Tools. Use Customize to choose formats. After installing, restart your DAW. In Logic, the plug-in appears under Audio Units › Basemental.
